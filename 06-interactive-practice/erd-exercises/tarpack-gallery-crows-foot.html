  • A customer can buy many artworks. A recorded artwork may remain unsold; once sold, it belongs to one customer through one sale transaction.
  • Every sale transaction records its date, time and total amount and is managed by exactly one staff member.
  • Each staff member manages at least one sale transaction.
  • A manager is a staff member, monitors at least one staff member, and a staff member can have at most one manager.
Modelling note: CUSTOMER and SALES_TRANSACTION require the design identifiers CustomerID and TransactionID, which are not named explicitly in the question. SALES_TRANSACTION avoids the reserved word TRANSACTION. TransactionID in ARTWORK is nullable while an artwork remains unsold. ManagerID is a self-referencing, nullable foreign key because “can only be monitored by one” sets a maximum but does not state that every staff member must already have a manager.
