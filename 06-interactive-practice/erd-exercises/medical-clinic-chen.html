Chen Notation: Medical Clinic

Model appointments and treatment details using ordinary Chen relationships.

Practice Studio Revision Hub
Question context and notation used
  • Doctors and patients meet through appointments. Each appointment identifies one doctor and one patient.
  • An appointment records its date, time and status.
  • An appointment may include zero or many treatments; a treatment may be used in zero or many appointments.
  • Dosage, instructions and treatment duration describe a treatment within a particular appointment.
Modelling choice: the treatment details are attributes of the `INCLUDES` relationship. This keeps the answer in ordinary Chen notation and avoids inventing a `TREATMENT_DETAIL` entity or an unsupported composite key.
