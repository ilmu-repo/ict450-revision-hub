  • Doctors and patients are connected through appointments; each appointment stores date, time and status.
  • A doctor can attend many patients and a patient can be treated by multiple doctors.
  • An appointment may include multiple treatments.
  • Dosage, instructions and treatment duration describe a treatment as used in one appointment, not the reusable treatment definition.
Modelling note: APPOINTMENT_TREATMENT resolves the Appointment-Treatment M:N relationship and stores its three detail attributes. The question never mentions billing, so no BILL table or billing relationship belongs in this model.
