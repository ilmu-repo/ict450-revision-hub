Open this question (PDF)ERD study notes

  • A swimmer can register for multiple events in one competition; each registration is supervised by exactly one official.
  • Registration must therefore identify the swimmer, event, competition and supervising official.
  • Performance belongs to a registered event and records round name, round date, competition time and position.
  • Each competition takes place at exactly one venue; a venue may host many competitions over time.
Modelling note: CompetitionID and RegistrationID are necessary identifiers even though the narrative does not list them. PERFORMANCE uses `(RegistrationID, RoundName)` as its composite key; repeating SwimmerID there would be redundant because Registration already identifies the swimmer. Crow's Foot shows “many”; the separate Part B(b) statement supplies the numeric maximum of 100 registrations per official.
