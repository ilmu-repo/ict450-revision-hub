  • A customer can buy many artworks. A recorded artwork may remain unsold; once sold, it belongs to one customer through one sale transaction.
  • Every staff member manages at least one transaction; each transaction has one managing staff member.
  • A transaction includes one or more artworks; each artwork can be sold only once.
  • Each artwork has one artist. An artist may be recorded before any artwork is entered.
  • A manager must monitor at least one staff member; a staff member may have at most one manager.
Modelling note: CustomerID and TransactionID are necessary design identifiers not named explicitly in the question. Both ends of MONITORS use STAFF, so the role labels Manager and Monitored Staff are required. `(0,1)` is used for monitored staff because “can only” states a maximum, not a mandatory minimum.
